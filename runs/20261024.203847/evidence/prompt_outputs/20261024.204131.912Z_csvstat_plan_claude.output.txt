kind:         capability
phase:        1

accepts:      st-005
stack:        go.md
provides:     profile.NumericStats
depends:      type-inference
acceptance:   yes
instructions: |
  Use math/big exact values and round once, half away from zero, when writing. Integer sum is a
  plain integer; number sum is two places; mean and median are two places for both types. Median is

  fields empty.
